Before you ask

Is this a good first trip to Japan?

Yes. Most guests are first-timers. Kyoto and Nara is the easiest start, and the guide handles every train and reservation.

Do I need to speak Japanese?

No. Your guide translates, and we write out the few phrases that are worth learning.

How much walking is there?

Most routes involve 5–8 km a day at an easy pace. Kumano Kodo is the exception, and its page lists the distances day by day.

What does the price include?

Lodging, breakfasts and most dinners, local transport, entry fees and your guide. International flights and lunches are not included.
